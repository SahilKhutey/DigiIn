## 2. Persona Profiles

### A. The Sovereign Citizen (Rahul Sharma — `DIN-DEMO-001`)
- **Profile**: 18-year-old high school graduate from Chhattisgarh applying for undergraduate university scholarships.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Frustrated by spending hours at internet cafes scanning marksheets, caste certificates, and income tax documents.
  - Anxious about uploading unredacted Aadhaar cards to third-party scholarship portals.
- **DigiIn Goal**: Complete applications in 2 minutes on a mobile phone with full control over what is disclosed.

### B. The University Scholarship Officer (`ORG-DEMO-001`)
- **Profile**: Administrative verification officer at the University of Delhi reviewing 15,000 scholarship applications annually.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Overwhelmed by backlogs of blurred PDF scans and manual phone calls to verify marks.
  - High legal liability and storage cost for storing hundreds of gigabytes of citizen PII scans.
- **DigiIn Goal**: Receive instant mathematical verification proofs (`VERIFIED ✓`) with zero document storage overhead.

### C. The Authoritative Credential Issuer (`ISSUER-DEMO-001`)
- **Profile**: State Board of Secondary Education & State Revenue Department.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Besieged by constant verification inquiry letters and counterfeit paper certificates.
- **DigiIn Goal**: Issue digitally signed, tamper-evident credentials once, enabling decentralized verification across any accredited institution.
