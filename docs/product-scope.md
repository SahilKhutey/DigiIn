# Product scope: DigiIn Document Lifecycle Platform

## Product promise

DigiIn helps citizens receive, upload, verify, correct, version, recover and share trusted documents with clear provenance, consent and auditability.

The first working slice is recovery: when an official document is not available, DigiIn explains the likely failed stage, the organisation responsible for that stage, what the citizen can safely do next, and when to escalate.

## Long-term product model

DigiIn is a trusted document lifecycle network connecting citizens, government issuers, government verifiers and requesters. It must support born-digital records and legacy paper records without blurring the difference between an uploaded file and an authoritative record.

## In scope (MVP)

- Document-recovery diagnostic for a fictional issued document.
- Plain-language status timeline and retry guidance.
- Issuer, identity-match, retrieval, consent and callback failure classifications.
- Accessibility-first, mobile-responsive citizen journey.
- Synthetic diagnostic data and a mock adapter boundary.

## Future platform scope

- Citizen-uploaded documents with clear unverified status.
- Government or issuer verification of citizen-submitted documents.
- Legacy physical record digitization and historical verification.
- Document correction, replacement, appeals and version history.
- Independent verification by authorised requesters with citizen consent.
- Minimum-disclosure sharing of verification results and selected claims.

## Out of scope (MVP)

- Credential collection, Aadhaar/OTP processing, or storage of identity documents.
- Direct access to DigiLocker, UIDAI, education-board, or other government systems.
- Automated eligibility or legal determinations.
- AI-made final government verification, correction or rejection decisions.
- Replacing official grievance and verification channels.

## Primary outcome

Increase document trust, recovery success and safe reuse while reducing repeated restarts, generic failure messages and unnecessary disclosure of raw documents.
