# Product scope: DigiIn Recovery Layer

## Product promise

When an official document is not available, DigiIn explains the likely failed stage, the organisation responsible for that stage, what the citizen can safely do next, and when to escalate.

## In scope (MVP)

- Document-recovery diagnostic for a fictional issued document.
- Plain-language status timeline and retry guidance.
- Issuer, identity-match, retrieval, consent and callback failure classifications.
- Accessibility-first, mobile-responsive citizen journey.
- Synthetic diagnostic data and a mock adapter boundary.

## Out of scope (MVP)

- Credential collection, Aadhaar/OTP processing, or storage of identity documents.
- Direct access to DigiLocker, UIDAI, education-board, or other government systems.
- Automated eligibility or legal determinations.
- Replacing official grievance and verification channels.

## Primary outcome

Increase end-to-end document recovery success while reducing repeated restarts and generic “something went wrong” experiences.
